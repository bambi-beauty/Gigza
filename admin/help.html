Help Center

Find answers to common questions about GIGZA

Getting Started

What is GIGZA?
GIGZA is a South African platform that connects event organizers with creative professionals like DJs, photographers, and videographers.
Is GIGZA free to join?
Yes! Joining as a creative or organizer is completely free. GIGZA only takes a small commission when a booking is completed.
How do I create an account?
Click "Join as Creative" or "Find a DJ" on our homepage. Fill in your details, verify your email, and you're ready to go!

For Event Organizers

How do I book a DJ?
Search for DJs in your area using location filters. Browse their profiles, check ratings, and send a booking request. For urgent needs, use Emergency Gig Mode.
What is Emergency Gig Mode?
Need a DJ in minutes? Post your event details (location, time, budget) and available DJs nearby can accept instantly. First to accept gets the gig!
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit cards, debit cards, and mobile payments through our secure payment system.
Can I cancel a booking?
Yes, but cancellation policies vary by creative. Always check their cancellation terms before booking. Deposits may be refundable depending on notice time.

For Creatives (DJs & Artists)

How do I get bookings?
Complete your profile with quality photos, videos, and music samples. The better your portfolio, the more likely organizers will book you. New creatives get a "Rising Talent" boost!
How do I get paid?
Payments are processed through the platform. Once a gig is completed, funds are released to your account within 3-5 business days.
What's the commission fee?
GIGZA takes a small commission from each completed booking. The exact percentage is shown before you accept any gig.
How do I qualify for Emergency Gig Mode?
Emergency Mode is for experienced, reliable DJs with good ratings. After completing several successful bookings, you may be invited to join Emergency Mode.

Safety & Trust

Are creatives verified?
Yes! We verify profiles and may request ID confirmation. Users with verified badges have completed our verification process.
What if something goes wrong?
You can report issues within 24 hours of the event. Our dispute resolution team will review both sides and help reach a fair outcome.
