Safety Tips

Stay safe and have a great experience on GIGZA

For Event Organizers

  • Always communicate through GIGZA's messaging system
  • Confirm all event details before booking (time, location, equipment)
  • Never pay outside the platform — we can't protect off-platform payments
  • Check creative's ratings and reviews before booking
  • Test equipment setup before the event starts
  • Get contact numbers as backup

For Creatives

  • Confirm venue address and access details in advance
  • Share your equipment and setup requirements early
  • Arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled time
  • Keep records of all communication through the platform
  • Rate organizers after events to build community trust
  • Save emergency contacts for the venue

Payment Safety

  • All payments should go through GIGZA's secure system
  • Never accept or send money outside the platform
  • Deposits are protected by our escrow system
  • Report any suspicious payment requests immediately
  • Keep screenshots of payment confirmations

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Requests to communicate outside GIGZA
  • Pressure to pay with cash or unreceipted transfers
  • Incomplete or suspicious profiles
  • Last-minute location changes
  • Requests for personal financial information

General Tips

  • Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, don't proceed
  • Share your event location with a friend or family member
  • Keep emergency contacts handy on your phone
  • Report any suspicious behavior to GIGZA immediately
  • Review users honestly to help the community

Venue Safety

  • Visit the venue beforehand if possible
  • Identify emergency exits and power sources
  • Have a backup plan for equipment failure
  • Keep valuables secure and out of sight
  • Know who to contact for venue issues

Emergency Situations

  • For immediate danger, call local emergency services first (10111 in South Africa)
  • Then report the incident to GIGZA
  • GIGZA is not responsible for incidents at event venues
  • Please ensure venues have proper security and emergency protocols
