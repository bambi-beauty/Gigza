Dispute Resolution

Fair and transparent process for when things go wrong

How it works

1

Report Issue

Within 24 hours of event end time

2

Provide Evidence

Photos, videos, messages

3

GIGZA Reviews

Both sides examined within 48 hours

4

Final Decision

Outcome communicated to both parties

What we review

  • Booking confirmation and event details
  • Communication between organizer and creative
  • Photo and video evidence provided
  • Previous ratings and history of both parties
  • Any prior disputes or warnings

Possible outcomes

Full Refund

Organizer receives 100% back. Creative didn't show up or completely failed to deliver.

Partial Refund

Organizer receives percentage back based on what was delivered.

No Refund

Creative keeps full payment. Organizer's complaint was unfounded.

Important notes

  • Disputes must be filed within 24 hours of event end time
  • Payment is held until dispute is resolved
  • Both parties will be notified of the decision
  • Our decision is final and binding
  • Repeated disputes may affect your account standing
